## Listening to the sector in the regions

Each region presents different conditions for transport: corridors, costs, infrastructure, journey times and operational needs. That is why these meetings are valuable. They let us hear directly from the people who move freight and compare solutions with the problems they face every day.

**COLFECAR en tu Región** brings knowledge, experience and conversation together around an essential question: how can transport become more productive, better informed and more connected to business realities?

## Data that helps people decide

At ATIEMPPO, we work to turn scattered information into useful tools. Analytics and artificial intelligence make sense when they reduce friction, make knowledge more accessible and help people make better decisions.

Taking part in this event in Cali lets us share what we are building, learn from the sector's questions and keep our work connected to operations.

We continue bringing technology, data and logistics knowledge to conversations where they can lead to concrete results.
