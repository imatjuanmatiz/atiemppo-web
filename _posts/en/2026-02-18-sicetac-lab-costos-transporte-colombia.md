SICETAC-LAB was born to solve a concrete friction: in many operations, calculating transport costs is still slow, manual and hard to trace.

## What problem we solve

- Long times to quote or validate a route/vehicle cost.
- Different interpretations of the regulation.
- Little traceability to audit decisions.

## What we did

We designed an agent that combines:

1. The regulatory context of transport in Colombia.
2. Operational variables by route.
3. Auditable calculation logic.

The result is a useful answer in seconds, not a static report.

## What changes for operations

- Better decision speed.
- Less rework between teams.
- Homogeneous and verifiable judgment.

## Next step

Keep expanding LatAm coverage and connect route-optimization recommendations to the daily operational flow.
