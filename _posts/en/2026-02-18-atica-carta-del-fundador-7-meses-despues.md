# 🚛 Founder's Letter: 7 months later

Dear community,

A little over seven months ago we decided to do something that seemed simple, but that actually meant changing a dynamic deeply rooted in the transport sector.

We wanted to transform the conversation.

At that moment, cargo transport in Colombia had information.  
It had platforms.  
It had dashboards.  
It had filters.

But it didn't have conversation.

And that's where ATICA was born.

---

## 🌱 More than 7 months, more than 1,400 unique users

Today, after more than seven months of life, with more than **1,400 unique users** and thousands of technical conversations, we can say that what began as a tool became something much bigger.

What we consider a true milestone isn't the number of users.

It's having made the conversation about transport move:

from pages with filters and charts,  
to a space where the user can **ask, compare, analyze and understand**, in a unique way, their cost possibilities and indicators.

ATICA allowed logistics to stop being just visualization and become dialogue.

It's no longer just about seeing a number. It's about understanding it.

It's no longer just about downloading a report. It's about exploring scenarios.

It's no longer just about checking a cost. It's about understanding the impact of each variable: logistics hours, territorial balance, on-route competitiveness, market value.

And that changes the way you decide.

---

## 🙌 Thanks to those who made this possible

None of this we did alone.

We want to deeply thank:

- The transport companies that trusted from day one.
- The cargo generators who decided to try a new way of analyzing.
- The consultants and analysts who challenged us with hard questions.
- The owners, operators and technicians who shared their real experience with us.

We learned with you. We drew from every conversation. We listened to every concern. We adjusted every detail.

ATICA wasn't designed in isolation. It was built with the collective intelligence of the sector.

And that's why today we can say it isn't just a tool. It's a shared creation.

---

## 🧠 What we achieved together

We made it possible for transport to:

- Compare technical cost vs market value.
- Analyze company concentration on a route.
- Understand territorial imbalances.
- Interpret the real impact of logistics hours.
- Connect regulation with operation.

We made the conversation more technical, deeper and more strategic.

But above all, we achieved something that wasn't on any roadmap:

We got the sector to start speaking with evidence.

---

## 🔮 The future arrives to change

And now comes the next evolution.

All this accumulated experience — all these conversations, all these patterns, all these questions — can't stay only in analysis.

ATICA evolves toward agents.

Agents that don't just provide data, but take action.

Agents that:

- Detect risks before they impact profitability.
- Suggest strategic decisions.
- Anticipate imbalances.
- Model future scenarios.
- Propose more efficient routes.
- Alert about regulatory risks.

The next step isn't to consult information.

It's to activate intelligence.

It isn't just to visualize data.

It's to turn it into automatic, strategic and timely decisions.

---

## 🚀 What's coming

We're convinced that transport needs more than platforms.

It needs assistants. It needs copilots. It needs specialized agents that understand its language and its complexity.

That's the future of ATICA.

And we want to build it with you.

Thank you for trusting. Thank you for asking. Thank you for challenging us. Thank you for taking part.

This is only the beginning.

With gratitude,
