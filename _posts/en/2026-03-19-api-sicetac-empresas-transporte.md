In many transport companies, the reference value of a route still lives in manual lookups, loose spreadsheets or people who carry the knowledge in their heads.

That model no longer measures up.

That's why at ATIEMPPO we're turning our **SICETAC API** into a product: a modern layer for companies to connect their systems, their agents and their teams to reference-cost calculation in a faster, more consistent and far more useful way for daily operations.

## What this layer enables

- Connect the TMS, CRM, ERP or internal tools to SICETAC value lookups.
- Feed conversational agents to answer route questions in natural language.
- Bring reference costs to commercial teams that need to react faster.
- Give operations more context for trip tracking, profitability and control.
- Help the finance area have a clearer base for cost tracking, budgets and margin conversations.

## Why it matters

When the reference value doesn't flow well inside the company, each area ends up working with different versions of the same problem.

Sales quotes with one logic.
Operations reviews with another.
Finance analyzes later, when it's already too late.

A well-built integration layer helps reduce that friction. It doesn't replace business judgment, but it does provide a common base so decisions are made with more speed and better traceability.

## What makes this approach different

We aren't thinking of the API as an isolated product for developers.

We're thinking of it as useful infrastructure for transport companies that want to connect systems, enable agents, automate recurring queries and modernize the way their teams understand the cost of a route.

Also, this layer is born with two practical advantages:

- it can respond via API for direct integrations
- it can serve as a backend for agents and conversational experiences
