This note gathers the ATIEMPPO updates published in February and March 2026.

## ATICA: the agent that understands the Colombian transport market

ATICA isn't a generic chatbot. It's an agent specialized in Colombian transport, designed to understand the sector's real context.

## Design principle

Context first, then answer.

That's why ATICA prioritizes:

- Local market terminology.
- The current regulatory framework.
- The operational reality of transport companies.

## What we achieved

- More precise and actionable answers.
- Less time searching for scattered information.
- Greater consistency across teams.

## What's next

Integrations with more operational sources so ATICA doesn't just answer, but recommends the best next step for each scenario.

## AEO: why visibility in AI is already a competitive advantage

Search is changing. In many sectors, the first point of contact is no longer Google: it's an AI assistant.

## What does this mean for a brand?

If your company isn't well structured to be understood by AI models, it simply doesn't show up in the conversation.

## From SEO to AEO/GEO

- **SEO**: optimizing for traditional search engines.
- **AEO/GEO**: optimizing so generative engines understand, cite and recommend your brand.

## What we focus on

1. Clarity of value proposition.
2. Semantic structure of content.
3. Authority and consistency signals.
4. Assets ready to be interpreted by AI.

## Expected result

More presence in AI-generated answers and a higher chance of being considered when the client is deciding.

## SICETAC al Instante Web: a simple interface for a complex query

A cost query can be technically complex and still feel simple to whoever uses it.

That's what we aim for with **SICETAC al Instante Web**: a clear experience to look up routes, vehicle configuration and body type without exposing the system's internal complexity.

## What we wanted to avoid

- Bare forms.
- Experiences that look like a demo and not a product.
- Interfaces that generate more friction than trust.

## What we're building

- A visual layer aligned with ATIEMPPO.
- Fast access to SICETAC routes.
- An experience connected with WhatsApp, GPTs and editorial content.

## What's next

Keep connecting the web experience with more operational signals and with the rest of the ecosystem.

## GPT El Dato Logístico: our specialized assistant in ChatGPT

Not all questions are answered the same way.

Some questions don't need a calculator. They need context, judgment, memory of the sector and a well-guided conversation.

That's why we opened the **El Dato Logístico GPT**.

## What it aims to solve

- Quick doubts with context.
- Conversations about market, logistics and data.
- A more accessible way to interact with the knowledge we've been accumulating.

## What makes it different

It isn't a generic GPT. It's born connected to the editorial and product ecosystem we're building.

## El Dato Logístico: an editorial layer to think better about logistics, transport and data

**El Dato Logístico** isn't just a newsletter. It's an editorial layer.

We want it to work as a bridge between data, judgment, market, tools and conversation with the community.

## What it does

- Translates market signals.
- Brings complex ideas down to clearer decisions.
- Feeds ATIEMPPO's product ecosystem.

## What we don't want

- Repeating news without judgment.
- Publishing empty content.
- Artificially separating content, product and community.

## La Comunidad: the depth layer of El Dato Logístico

Open content is good for attracting and orienting. But there's a point where depth is needed.

For that we're opening **La Comunidad**, El Dato Logístico's space on Skool.
