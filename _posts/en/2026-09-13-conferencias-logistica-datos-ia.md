As a speaker, I bring business, industry and academic audiences a conversation built on more than 25 years of work in logistics, transport, public policy, data and technology products across Latin America.

## Five conversations for teams that need to make better decisions

### Freight transport and costs

How to read references, networks, exceptions and cost structures so that data supports decisions this week, as well as informing a report.

### Data ethics in logistics

What to measure, what to disclose, what not to invent, and how to develop the judgment an organization needs to trust the information it uses.

### Artificial intelligence applied to transport

Moving from chat to an agent that acts with a purpose, authorized sources, clear limits and human review. The discussion starts with real tasks.

### Public policy and regional evidence

What national logistics surveys, transport projects and regional experience teach us when governments, companies and multilateral organizations must act with incomplete information.

### Leading teams that work with data and AI

How to support change while retaining human judgment: deciding what to automate, what to review and which capabilities a team needs to use a tool well.

## The talk is built around a question

A conference talk has a different shape from a session for an executive team. I work with the organizer to understand the audience, the context and the decision the conversation should help clarify.

Formats can include a **keynote, panel, moderated discussion or private workshop**. The depth and duration are agreed with the organizer.

## What remains after the event

- A common language for discussing the problem.
- More precise questions for the team.
- Criteria for distinguishing signals, data and opinion.
- A way to connect the conversation to a decision, a test or a next step.

A talk can be the starting point. When the question needs further work, ATIEMPPO can support the analysis, build a tool or turn the discussion into an applied learning experience.
