ATICA isn't a generic chatbot. It's an agent specialized in Colombian transport, designed to understand the sector's real context.

## Design principle

Context first, then answer.

That's why ATICA prioritizes:

- Local market terminology.
- The current regulatory framework.
- The operational reality of transport companies.

## What we achieved

- More precise and actionable answers.
- Less time searching for scattered information.
- Greater consistency across teams.

## What's next

Integrations with more operational sources so ATICA doesn't just answer, but recommends the best next step for each scenario.
