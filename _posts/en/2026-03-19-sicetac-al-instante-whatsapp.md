There are moments when a person doesn't need an entire platform. They need a fast answer.

That's why we opened **SICETAC al Instante on WhatsApp**: a more direct way to check a route from your phone, without getting into a technical interface.

## What problem it solves

- People in operations who need speed.
- One-off queries that don't justify opening a full platform.
- Teams that prefer a familiar, conversational channel.

## How to use it

You write it like this:

`origin a destination`

Don't forget to put the `a` (Spanish for "to").

## Why it matters

It isn't just one more channel. It's a way to reduce friction between the question and the answer.
