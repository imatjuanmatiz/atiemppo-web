How we work

  1. Understand the need. Identify the decision, process or capability to improve.
  2. Review the inputs. Agree on sources, available information and limitations.
  3. Define the work. Set objectives, activities, deliverables, responsibilities and commercial terms.

Start through the contact form or email. Follow-up channels and timelines are agreed for each project.

Frequently asked questions

What does ATIEMPPO do?

We combine consulting, transport intelligence and AI productivity. Deliverables may include analysis, structured data, scenarios, recommendations or practical learning, depending on the agreed scope.

How does ATIEMPPO combine consulting and technology?

Our consulting defines the need, analyzes alternatives and supports decisions. Data products and tools support that work; technical development, integrations and automation require an additional defined scope.

How does a SICETAC database differ from network analysis?

The database provides structured references for your team. Network analysis matches them to your routes and operating weights to produce a tailored view.

How does an engagement start?

Tell us what you need to decide or improve. We review the available information and prepare a proposal with objectives, work, deliverables, responsibilities and commercial terms.

What does the AI program provide?

The AI productivity program covers needs prioritization, project definition, supported testing and documentation. Your organization retains responsibility for decisions and source permissions.
