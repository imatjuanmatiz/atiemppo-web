Our founder

Juan Pablo Matiz

Applied researcher, consultant and educator

Explore his work →

An economist with more than 25 years of experience in logistics and transport across Latin America. His career began in business operations and expanded into consulting, research and public policy.

Through Imétrica, independent consulting and ATIEMPPO, he has worked with businesses, governments and multilateral organizations. At ATIEMPPO, he also develops data products and AI tools to support teams in their work.

Teaching and Profe Bruno are ways to share that experience and learn from other people's questions.
