More time for action, grounded in judgment.

Our approach

AI matters when it improves the way a team works.

We combine sector knowledge, traceable sources, tools and human review. Agents accelerate the work while people remain responsible for the decision.

01 · Time

We reduce scattered searches, rework and repetitive tasks so attention returns to what matters.

02 · Judgment

We make visible the assumptions, limits, sources and questions behind a recommendation.

03 · Action

The result should help a team prioritize, discuss, budget, activate or choose the next step.

04 · Continuity

We document learning and build capabilities that can be repeated and improved with the team.

Where we come from

A mix of logistics, data, technology and applied learning.

“Technology matters when it gives a difficult decision better context and less friction.”

ATIEMPPO grew from working close to the questions that shape transport: what a route costs, how a network changes, what information is missing, which corridor deserves attention and how a decision can remain useful over time.

That work led us to combine consulting, structured information, applications, living reports, learning and agents. They are separate capabilities that can come together around each company's priority.

We are building a new layer of work for logistics and transport: teams that query their data better, learn to direct agents and turn decisions into memory, processes and action.
