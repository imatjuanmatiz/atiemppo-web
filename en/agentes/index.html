How it comes together

Agents execute. Architecture gives them direction.

Codex, Profe Bruno, OpenClaw and the orchestrator have different roles. Together they form a workbench where a question can become a plan, task, deliverable and reusable memory.

01 · ObjectiveDefine the decision or task that should improve and how success will be recognized.
02 · SourcesConnect the organization's knowledge, tools and authorized sources.
03 · BoundariesAssign a role, permissions, human review and delivery format.
04 · ContinuityRecord learning so the process can continue without starting over.
