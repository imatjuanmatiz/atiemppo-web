What we do with SICETAC

SICETAC is the Efficient Cost System for Automotive Freight Transport, from the Ministry of Transport. It is the official cost reference for road freight transport in Colombia.

ATIEMPPO helps you look up that reference, see how it moves and use it on a route, on a network or with a team. We are independent of the Ministry: this is not the official application and it is not a certification.
