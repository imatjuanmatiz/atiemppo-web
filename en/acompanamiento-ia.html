Train your team and support its AI projects.

Learn, apply and retain the capability

1. Choose a challenge

Agree on a task, sources, an owner and acceptance criteria.

2. Practice with AI

3. Build and test

5. Continue with new projects

Guidance from ATIEMPPO and continuity within your team

Juan Pablo Matiz and the ATIEMPPO team provide experience, methodology and review. Supporting agents help organize context, meeting notes and documentation between sessions within the agreed scope and permissions.

The training includes guided construction of learning agents within the agreed scope, alongside workshops, tests, documentation and follow-up. Your organization retains responsibility for decisions. Specialist technical development, integrations, automation and system connections are defined and quoted separately.
