Frequently asked questions

Does database access include route matching?

The database and network analysis are different services. Route matching uses your route master; the Network Index also requires a weighting variable for your operation.

What information is needed for a network analysis?

Origins, destinations, vehicle configurations and a variable such as trip frequency, volume or cost. We review available fields and limitations before defining the work.

Can we begin with a specific question?

Yes. Describe what you need to decide and the information available. The proposal defines the appropriate service and its deliverables.

Are system integrations included?

Technical integrations, developments and automation are separate scopes. A strategic presentation is included at no additional cost when another ATIEMPPO service is contracted, within the agreed scope.

We agree on objectives, deliverables, responsibilities and working hours. Commercial terms are provided separately according to scope.
