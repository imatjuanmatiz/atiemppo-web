Getting started

Your task gives us
a place to start.

Tell us what you want to do and what you have tried. We can then suggest the support or session that fits your needs.

During practice, you learn to explain your task, provide the materials the AI needs and check the result. When something goes wrong, we work through what was missing and how to improve it.

If the task repeats, we can explore how to organize it and which parts an agent could handle.

Ideas to get started

You might have a task like one of these.

“I have a file I need to understand.”

Learn to ask questions about your data, explore what it shows and check the calculations.

“I want to improve my reports.”

Work with your sources and organize what you need to say while keeping your own voice.

“I repeat the same task every week.”

Describe the steps, identify where AI could help and check the result.

These examples can help you choose a task to learn with. We agree on the scope around what you want to work on.
