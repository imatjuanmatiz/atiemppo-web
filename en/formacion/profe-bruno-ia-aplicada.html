Applied learning · Profe Bruno

Learn AI by working on real cases.

Profe Bruno is an ATIEMPPO initiative for people and teams who want to learn artificial intelligence through real problems, clear language and useful exercises. The central idea is simple: AI is learned better when it connects to work someone already needs to do.

Talk about a learning experienceSee live cases

The practical leap

Move from using a tool to building a way of working.

Profe Bruno supports the transition from asking an AI for an answer to directing work with context, sources, limits and review.

01 · QUESTIONS

Ask better questions

Frame a need, a decision or a task so the work has a clear purpose.

02 · CONTEXT

Work with files and sources

Bring in the documents, examples, rules and references that shape a useful result.

03 · ROUTINES

Build repeatable workflows

Turn frequent work into an organized routine that can be reviewed and improved.

04 · AGENTS

Direct specialized agents

Understand what an agent receives, what it does, which tools it uses and when it asks for help.

05 · REVIEW

Check before deciding

Recognize when an answer sounds plausible but still needs evidence and human judgment.

06 · CONTINUITY

Keep learning in the work

Leave a method and a next step that the person or team can continue using.

What learning can include

A route shaped around the task you want to improve.

PeopleA practical first step for professionals, analysts, entrepreneurs or students.
ExecutivesSessions to identify opportunities, risks, priorities and governance questions.
TeamsWorkshops based on processes, cases and the information the team already handles.
CasesExamples from Bruno, road updates, living reports and sector information.
