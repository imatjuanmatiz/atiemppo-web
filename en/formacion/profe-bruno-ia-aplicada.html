Your own AI capabilities

Keep the capability to work with AI in your team.

We train people to choose tools, give instructions, check results and maintain what they build. Your team retains the knowledge: a method, sources, decisions and clear responsibilities to keep going.

We start with your experience and each participant’s level. An individual task can be a starting point; a team can learn to build and review agents around its own processes.

Learning by doing

From one task to a capability you can reuse.

  1. 1. Choose a challenge

    Define the task, available sources, a responsible person and what the result needs to achieve.

    A first task with a clear scope.

  2. 2. Practice with AI

    Learn to provide context, choose tools and check answers using materials you are allowed to use.

    Judgment to work and verify.

  3. 3. Build and test

    Create or adapt an agent with guidance: instructions, sources, boundaries and tests for the chosen task.

    A learning agent you can review.

  4. 4. Document and transfer

    Record how it works, what it needs, where it fails and who maintains it. A teammate practices using the guide.

    Knowledge that can be shared.

  5. 5. Continue with new projects

    Reuse what you have learned, review progress and agree on the specialist support needed for the next challenge.

    A next step with clear ownership.

What your team retains

A guide to keep working.

The task and its sources

The objective, approved materials, instructions and boundaries of the agent.

Tests and decisions

Reviewed examples, acceptance criteria and changes made to improve the result.

Use and maintenance

Steps to use it, an owner, update needs and future projects.

Transfer involves practice: another person uses the guide, explains its limits and proposes an improvement. We agree on these materials within the scope of the support.
