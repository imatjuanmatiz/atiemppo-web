Service 01 · Consulting supported by AI

We turn complex questions into decisions teams can defend and execute.

ATIEMPPO develops tailored consulting for transport, logistics and supply chain companies and teams. We combine sector expertise, data, specialized sources and artificial intelligence to accelerate analysis, preserve traceability and turn findings into concrete options for action.

The starting question

What decision will the company be able to make with greater clarity, evidence and confidence?

What we can solve

Consulting starts with the decision and the value we want to activate.

We work from a concrete question and organize the evidence needed to answer it. AI accelerates searches, comparisons and documentation; expert judgment reviews what matters and guides action.

01 · NETWORK

Locations and network scenarios

We compare location alternatives, current and future demand, nodes, corridors and service conditions.

02 · MARKET

Actors, companies and opportunities

We identify and prioritize companies, segments, logistics nodes and corridors using multiple sources and visible criteria.

03 · COSTS

Transport costs and scenarios

We analyze rates, cost structures, load balance, trips and scenarios to understand what can change a decision.

04 · OPERATIONS

Network and operations diagnostics

We identify opportunities in freight, safety, sustainability, data coverage and applied artificial intelligence.

05 · PROFILING

Market and actor profiles

We build focused readings of sectors and actors with secondary sources, dates, confidence levels and documented scope.

06 · NEXT STEP

From answer to continuity

When the analysis reveals it, we leave a path toward recurring data, specialized agents or new team capabilities.

Our method

Organized evidence. Expert judgment. Team activation.

The result is an explainable answer, with visible assumptions, sources and criteria, ready to become a decision conversation.

01

Focus

We reformulate the problem and agree on questions, scope, audience and acceptance criteria.

02

Design the evidence

We define sources, cuts, variables, assumptions, confidence levels and quality controls.

03

Build the analysis

We clean, cross, research, model and compare scenarios with traceability.

04

Contrast

We review findings, exceptions, plausibility and operational consequences with sector judgment.

05

Synthesize

We turn analysis into conclusions, options and recommendations that can be prioritized.

06

Activate

We work through the result with the team and record decisions, questions and next analyses.

Configurable outputs

The deliverable is designed around the decision.

Each project receives the combination of outputs that best helps the team understand, defend and activate the result.

“The client receives an explainable answer, organized evidence and a path to action.”
  • Executive decision brief
  • Technical and methodological appendix
  • Evidence matrix or consolidated base
  • Comparable scenarios and assumptions
  • Maps, actor or corridor profiles
  • Executive results session
  • Decision and data backlog
  • Memory or agentic layer, if contracted

How we protect the result

Every claim is ready to be reviewed.

We separate observed facts, estimates and scenarios. Sources, assumptions, criteria and missing data stay visible so the team can use the result with confidence.

Traceable sources and calculations

Each relevant conclusion connects to an identified source, calculation or assumption.

Confidence criteria

We present each finding according to its nature: observed fact, proxy, estimate or scenario.

Controlled scope

Interviews, field work, integrations, software and projections are quoted when included in scope.
