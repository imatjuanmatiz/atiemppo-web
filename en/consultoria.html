How we work

First, let's talk.
Then let's plan the study.

Tell us what is happening and what you need to decide. We review your information and agree on a proposal with a scope, timeline and deliverables.

We discuss the findings with you as the study progresses. At the end, we work through the conclusions with your team so they can understand the alternatives and their implications.

Depending on the project, you may receive a report, a dataset, maps or comparable scenarios. The proposal sets out what is included and what information is needed.
